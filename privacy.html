Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

Prep Studio is a study app for the WBPSC Industrial Development Officer exam. This page explains what information it uses and why.

Your access code and name

To open the course you enter a personal access code. Your name is stored inside the course data, encrypted with your code, so the app can greet you and show your name as a watermark. Your code is remembered in your browser on your device so you don't have to type it again. You can remove it at any time from More → Lock this device.

Your study progress (Google Drive)

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app saves your progress (lessons done, answers, flashcard schedule, notes) in one file in your own Google Drive, named prep-studio-ido-progress.json. The app asks only for the drive.file permission, which lets it see and change only files it created itself. It cannot see any other file in your Drive. We do not receive a copy of your progress; it goes directly between your browser and Google.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to save and load your own progress, is not sold, is not used for advertising, and is not shared with anyone.

If you don't sign in, your progress stays only in your browser on that device. You can export or import a backup file from More → Backup.

Payments

If you pay for an access code, you pay by UPI from your own UPI app; the app itself does not handle or store any payment details. When you send your payment screenshot on WhatsApp, we see what the screenshot shows (amount, UPI reference, and usually your name and UPI ID) and your WhatsApp number. We use these only to confirm the payment and send you your code, and keep them only as a record of the payment.

Other services the app contacts

  • Netlify, which hosts the site (it may keep standard server logs such as IP address).
  • Google sign-in and Google Drive, only if you sign in.
  • Google Fonts, to load the app's typefaces.

The app has no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

Deleting your data

Delete prep-studio-ido-progress.json from your Google Drive, and remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To remove your saved code from a device, use More → Lock this device. To ask us to delete your payment record or name, message us on WhatsApp.
